# Landing Page Copy Brief

## The Demo Context

This tool is being shown to decision-makers at boutique direct mail / catalog agencies as a pitch for a new digital service layer. The presenter has built this as an adaptation of work done in another engagement — the framing is "I've already solved this problem elsewhere, here's what it looks like applied to your world."

The audience is smart, experienced, and skeptical of digital hype. They run real businesses with real clients. They are not trying to become a tech company. The pitch is not "here is software." The pitch is "here is a capability you could offer."

---

## The Two Aha Moments to Engineer

**Aha #1 — "This is real."**
The landing page needs to project quiet confidence, not startup energy. No buzzwords. No "AI-powered" in the headline. The tool should feel like something a serious analyst built, not a demo someone cobbled together. The score ring, the five specialist dimensions, the data inputs panel — these are credibility signals. The copy just needs to not undermine them.

**Aha #2 — "Wait, we could do this for our clients."**
This one happens in conversation, not on the page — but the page can prime it. If the copy frames this as *a service delivery tool* rather than a website checker, the GM's brain starts doing the math before you say a word. The difference: "analyze any website" vs. "deliver a marketing assessment in 60 seconds."

---

## Tone

- Confident, not flashy
- Specific, not hype-y
- Feels like it belongs to a firm, not a side project
- Treats the user as a professional, not a consumer

---

## Hero Section

**Headline options** (short, no AI jargon):

- *Full Marketing Assessment. Sixty Seconds.*
- *Five-Dimension Marketing Review. Any Website.*
- *Instant Marketing Intelligence. Built for Practitioners.*
- *A Complete Marketing Read. Before the First Meeting.*

**Subhead options** (one sentence, frames it as a service tool):

- *Content, conversion, SEO, competitive positioning, and brand strategy — delivered simultaneously, scored, and ready to share.*
- *Five specialist analyses running in parallel. A scored report you can hand to a client.*
- *The marketing assessment your team used to spend a week on. Now it runs while you're on the phone.*

**Recommended pairing:**
> *Full Marketing Assessment. Sixty Seconds.*
> Five specialist analyses running in parallel. A scored report you can hand to a client.

---

## Input Area

Current: `"https://yourwebsite.com"` placeholder, "Run Audit" button, "No account required" footnote.

Suggested footnote swap:
- *"Takes 30–60 seconds. Downloads as a shareable report."* — surfaces the deliverable before they even run it.

The name/company fields are already doing work here — they signal this is a professional tool with some intentionality behind it, not a free toy.

---

## What to Avoid

- "AI-powered" — sounds like a product hunt submission
- "Revolutionary" / "cutting-edge" — instant credibility drain with this audience
- Lengthy feature lists before the input — get them to run it first, explain after
- Consumer SaaS patterns (star ratings, "Join 10,000 marketers!") — wrong register entirely

---

## The DM Adaptation Hook (for the conversation, not the page)

Once they've seen a digital audit run, the bridge to their world is:

> "The engine is the same. The data sources are different. Instead of page HTML and PageSpeed, you're feeding it mail file segments, response history, and format test results. Instead of SEO and conversion agents, you have list health and circulation strategy agents. The output — a scored report with prioritized recommendations — is identical."

This doesn't need to be on the page. It's the verbal close after the demo lands.

---

## Suggested Page Title / Meta Description

**Title:** `Marketing Intelligence — Full-Spectrum Website Analysis`

**Meta description:** `Five-dimension marketing assessment for any website. Content, conversion, SEO, competitive positioning, and brand strategy — scored and ready to share in under 60 seconds.`
